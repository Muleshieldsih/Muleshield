▬Proposed solution
The gap: a 1930 complaint tells you a crime happened. It does not tell you 
where the cash will leave the system, or when.
MuleShield AI turns that complaint into a ranked, time-bounded search: five 
ATMs, in order, with a countdown.
▬How it addresses the problem
Traces the money. Builds the transaction graph and follows the chain to the 
mule account still holding the funds.
Scores every account. A GraphSAGE network reads who-paid-whom, not just 
per-account behaviour — F1 0.8955.
Narrows the ground. 1,000 ATMs → 25 reachable → 5 worth searching, in 
7.5 ms.
Starts the clock. Median 42 minutes of warning before the predicted 
withdrawal.
▬Innovation and uniqueness
Ranked retrieval, not a guess — a list a field team can cover, never one predicted ATM.
Graph-native detection — the graph is worth +5 F1 points over the best non-graph model.
Published honestly — the console shows its own baselines and limits on screen.
Zero new data — runs on the fields an NCRP complaint already carries.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
4
@SIH Idea submission- Template
[TEAM
NAME]
▬Feasibility
Technical: already built and measured — 291 passing tests, 7.5 ms inference, 
runs on one CPU container.
Data: consumes the fields NCRP already collects; no new citizen-facing process.
Operational: delivers into the existing 1930 workflow — a queue, a case, an 
action, an audit trail.
Economic: open-source stack end to end; one container, no GPU at inference.
▬Challenges and how we handle them
Leakage inflating results. Found in our own first build (98.5% claimed). Rebuilt 
the generator, re-baselined, and added a test that fails if a retracted figure 
returns.
A model that looks confident and is wrong. Isotonic calibration, verified against 
held-out labels by a dedicated test.
Synthetic training data. Generated to be hard — 2% label noise, overlapping 
classes, complaint-level splits. Pipeline retrains on real I4C data unchanged.
Acting on a wrong location. The system returns candidates, never a verdict; the 
UI and every dispatch alert say so.
Single-process state. Documented limit — case state is in-memory; a database 
swap is a day's work when multi-user is needed.
Viability — measured, not asserted

IMPACT AND BENEFITS
5
@SIH Idea submission- Template
[TEAM
NAME]
▬Who it affects
Victims of cyber-financial fraud — 1930 / NCRP callers, 
nationwide.
Cyber-crime police acting inside the golden hour.
I4C and MHA — pattern intelligence across cases, not one 
complaint at a time.
Banks receiving a specific account to freeze.
▬Benefits
Social: recovery becomes possible for ordinary complainants, 
not only large-value cases.
Economic: cash stopped before withdrawal is money 
recovered; narrower freezes mean fewer legitimate accounts 
locked.
Operational: finite patrol capacity aimed at 5 locations instead 
of a district.
Governance: every freeze and dispatch attributed, 
timestamped and auditable.
Strategic: recurring cash-out ATMs surface across cases — the 
mule network, not just the incident.
Impact figures are held-out measurements, not projections.
